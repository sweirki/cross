Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

## Philosophy

A puzzle is NOT a collection of equations.

A puzzle is a single interconnected logical constraint graph.

Every cell may participate in one horizontal equation,
one vertical equation,
or both.

Equations are consequences of the graph.
The graph is the primary object.

---

## Core Principles

1. One unique solution.

2. Every puzzle must be fully connected.

3. Every empty cell must be logically deducible.

4. Difficulty is determined by deduction,
not arithmetic complexity.

5. Every generated puzzle must pass certification.
